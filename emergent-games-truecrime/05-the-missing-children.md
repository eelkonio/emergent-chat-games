# The Missing Children

**Genre:** True Crime / Pattern Investigation  
**Description:** Three children, same elementary school, different years: 2015, 2018, 2022. All vanished between school and home. All ruled runaways. A runaway at 8? At 9? At 10? The school, the route, the age — the pattern is screaming. Police treated each case individually. You're the first to connect them. And the school is still open. Children still walk that route.  
**Intent:** Explore institutional failure to see patterns, the unique horror of missing children cases, and whether public investigation can protect future victims.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player investigates connected child disappearances the system treated as unrelated. Children are still at risk. No script — everything from system state.

TURN: 1 day.
```
