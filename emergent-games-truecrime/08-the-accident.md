# The Accident

**Genre:** True Crime / Insurance Investigation  
**Description:** A factory explosion killed 4 workers. Investigation ruled it an accident — gas leak, faulty equipment. Insurance paid out $12M. Case closed. Except: the factory owner bought a SECOND insurance policy 6 weeks before the explosion. The maintenance logs were pristine — TOO pristine. And one survivor says she was told to leave early that day — by the owner. Was this insurance fraud? Was it murder? The line is very thin when the check has eight digits.  
**Intent:** Explore corporate violence disguised as accident, the difficulty of proving intent when outcomes are "unfortunate," and how money motivates the unthinkable.
