# The Serial

**Genre:** True Crime / Serial Killer Investigation  
**Description:** Three women killed across three states over 18 months. Different jurisdictions. Different police departments. Nobody connected them until you did — same strangulation method, same victim profile, same month gap between kills. You aired Episode 1 last week. Now the FBI is involved — and pissed you went public before them. The killer knows you're looking. Your audience is 800K and growing. And the pattern says the next one is due in 6 weeks.  
**Intent:** Explore the ethics of broadcasting danger, competition vs. cooperation with law enforcement, and whether podcast fame helps or hinders serial investigations.

---
