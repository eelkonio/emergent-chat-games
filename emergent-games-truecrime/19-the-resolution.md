# The Resolution

**Genre:** True Crime / Moral Dilemma  
**Description:** You know who killed David Park. The evidence is clear to you — circumstantial but overwhelming. Motive, opportunity, behavior pattern, everything aligns. But "clear to you" isn't "provable in court." The DA won't file charges. The suspect knows you know. They're living freely. Your audience knows your theory. And the victim's family is asking: "If you KNOW, why is nothing happening?" The answer is: the legal system has a threshold your evidence doesn't meet.  
**Intent:** Explore the gap between knowing and proving, vigilante justice through media, and whether exposing a suspect without legal consequences is justice or cruelty.

---

## Prompt

