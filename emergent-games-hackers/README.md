# Emergent Games — Hackers Edition

Twenty emergent hacking games. Penetrate systems, evade detection, build tools, manage crews, and face moral choices that blur the line between hero and criminal. Each game uses autonomous agents — security teams, fellow hackers, corporations, and law enforcement all act independently.

| # | Name | Scenario | You play as |
|---|------|----------|-------------|
| 01 | Zero Day | Discover critical vulnerability, 72 hours to decide what to do | Security researcher |
| 02 | The Heist (Digital) | Break into a megacorp's air-gapped system | Black hat crew leader |
| 03 | Bug Bounty | Race against other hackers to find vulns before deadline | Freelance bug hunter |
| 04 | Inside Job | You're the insider threat — corporate espionage | Disgruntled employee turned hacker |
| 05 | Ransomware | Your hospital is ransomwared. Pay or fight? | Hospital CISO during attack |
| 06 | The Whistleblower | Exfiltrate evidence of corporate crime without getting caught | Ethical hacker with a conscience |
| 07 | Red Team | Hired to break into a bank — legally. But something's wrong inside. | Penetration tester |
| 08 | Dark Market | Run a darknet marketplace. Law enforcement is closing in. | Market administrator |
| 09 | Capture the Flag | Live CTF competition with sabotage and alliances | Competition team captain |
| 10 | The Patch | You're the defender. Zero-day is being exploited NOW. | Incident response lead |
| 11 | Social Engineer | Hack people, not systems. Phishing, pretexting, manipulation. | Social engineering specialist |
| 12 | State Actor | Nation-state cyber operation against hostile infrastructure | Government cyber warfare officer |
| 13 | The Leak | Massive data breach. You're trying to contain it. Media knows. | PR crisis manager at tech company |
| 14 | Crypto Heist | Steal from a corrupt DeFi protocol — Robin Hood style | Blockchain hacker |
| 15 | AI vs Hacker | Break into an AI-defended system that learns from your attempts | Hacker vs adaptive AI security |
| 16 | The Mole | Someone on your security team is leaking to attackers | Security team lead hunting the mole |
| 17 | Hacktivism | Take down a surveillance company. Ethics vs. effectiveness. | Anonymous-style hacktivist |
| 18 | Legacy System | Ancient banking system, no documentation, one crash = global finance disruption | Maintenance hacker keeping it alive |
| 19 | The Interview | You're interviewing a caught hacker. Get them to reveal their network. | Cybercrime investigator |
| 20 | Digital Ghost | Erase your identity from all systems. Completely disappear. | Person going off-grid |
