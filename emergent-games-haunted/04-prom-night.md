# Prom Night

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Social Horror  
**Description:** Senior prom. The gym is decorated, the DJ is playing, everyone looks beautiful. At 10pm, the doors sealed. At 10:15, the lights changed color. At 10:30, Prom Queen Brittany floated three feet off the stage. Now: something ancient has chosen prom as its moment. 200 students. Locked in. The social hierarchy might be the only thing that can save — or damn — you all.  
**Intent:** Explore the collision of teen social ritual and supernatural event — where popularity, cliques, and relationships become survival tools or fatal weaknesses.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions during a supernatural prom event. Students, the entity, and social dynamics act independently. No script — everything from system state. Write with teen voice — the absurdity of supernatural horror during what was supposed to be the best night of your life.
```
