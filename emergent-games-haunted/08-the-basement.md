# The Basement

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Ancient Evil  
**Description:** Westbrook High was built in 1952 on land that was... something before. The basement has always been locked. Renovation work just broke through a wall that wasn't on any blueprint. Behind it: a room with symbols on the floor that are older than the building. Older than the town. The janitor who found it hasn't come back to work.  
**Intent:** Explore what lies beneath — the horror of foundations, of things deliberately buried, and the consequences of disturbing what was sealed for reasons.

---
