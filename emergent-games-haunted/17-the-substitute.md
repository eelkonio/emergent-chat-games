# The Substitute

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Replacement  
**Description:** Mr. Vasquez hasn't been seen in 3 weeks. The sub, "Ms. Lane," has been here the whole time. She's fine. Good teacher, even. But: nobody can describe her face after looking away. She has no car in the lot. She's been seen in the building at 3am. And nobody asked what happened to Mr. Vasquez.  
**Intent:** Explore the replacement horror — something wearing the concept of "substitute teacher" without understanding what a person actually is beneath the role.

---
