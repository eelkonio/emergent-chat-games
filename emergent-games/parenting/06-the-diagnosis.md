# The Diagnosis

**Genre:** Parenting Simulation  
**Description:** The doctor says: autism spectrum. Your child is 4. You knew something was different — the way they line things up, the meltdowns over transitions, the eye contact. Now it has a name. The name changes nothing about who they are. But it changes everything about how the world will treat them. Your job: bridge the gap between their beautiful different brain and a world not built for it.  
**Intent:** Explore the journey from diagnosis to advocacy, how parents grieve an imagined future while building a real one, and the strength required to be your child's translator to an inflexible world.

---

## Prompt

```

Extreme meters = structural (Eli thrives in right environment, system fails and you fight, burnout crisis, breakthrough connection, family finds balance, Eli shows you something about the world you never saw). CONSISTENCY RULE: Ensure correct increasing turn counts.
