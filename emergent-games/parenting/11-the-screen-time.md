# The Screen Time

**Genre:** Parenting Simulation  
**Description:** Your 11-year-old has a phone. It was for safety. Now it's an appendage. They scroll at breakfast, message under the table, watch videos until midnight. Their grades haven't dropped yet. Their friendships are partly online. You want to set limits but you also know: their social world lives in that device. Remove it and you remove them from their peers. Keep it and watch them disappear into glass.  
**Intent:** Explore the impossible negotiation of digital childhood, how technology reshapes attention and connection, and parenting in a world that didn't exist when you were a kid.

---

## Prompt

