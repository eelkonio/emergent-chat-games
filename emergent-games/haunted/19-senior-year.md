# Senior Year

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Institutional  
**Description:** Senior year. 47 days until graduation. Except: seniors keep getting held back. Mysterious incomplete credits appearing. Transfer paperwork "lost." College acceptances revoked by clerical errors. The school isn't letting seniors graduate. The school doesn't WANT to let anyone leave. Ever.  
**Intent:** Explore the school-as-predator — an institution that has been feeding on students for generations, keeping them, not letting them leave. Graduation is escape. The school prevents escape.

---
