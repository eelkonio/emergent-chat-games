# Detention

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Transformation  
**Description:** Detention. Room 104. 3:30pm. Five students, one teacher. Except: at 4pm the teacher left "to get coffee" and never came back. At 4:30 the hallway outside the door was... different. At 5pm you realized the school has changed. It's still a school. But it's not YOUR school anymore. And it's hungry.  
**Intent:** Explore the school-as-organism horror — where the familiar becomes predatory, and a building you've walked through every day reveals itself as something else entirely.
