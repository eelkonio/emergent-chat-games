# The Game

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Ludic Trap  
**Description:** Found in the library's basement storage: a board game with no brand, no instructions, no date. Handmade. Beautiful. Four students played it Tuesday after school. By Thursday, two haven't come to school. Their pieces on the board have moved — but nobody touched it. You're one of the four. Your piece is still on the board.  
**Intent:** Explore the game-as-entity — where playing is binding, the rules emerge through consequence, and the only way out might be to finish what you started.
