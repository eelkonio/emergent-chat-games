# The Play

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Performance  
**Description:** The fall play this year: "The Hollow Men" — found in the drama teacher's old files, author unknown. During the first full run-through, the auditorium temperature dropped 15 degrees. An audience member who wasn't there appeared in the back row. By Act 3, the actors aren't reading from the script anymore — the words are coming from somewhere else. Opening night is in 4 days.  
**Intent:** Explore performance as invocation — where art becomes ritual, repetition becomes summoning, and the show must NOT go on.

---

## Prompt

