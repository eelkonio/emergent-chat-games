# Spirit Week

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Manifestation  
**Description:** Spirit Week at Westbrook. Monday: Pajama Day. Tuesday: Decades Day. Wednesday: Superhero Day. Thursday: Fear Day. The themes are manifesting LITERALLY. Monday everyone fell asleep in 2nd period. Tuesday the school physically transformed to the 1950s for an hour. It's Wednesday now and people are developing actual powers. Thursday is "Fear Day." You have 24 hours before everyone's nightmares become real.  
**Intent:** Explore manifestation horror — where intention becomes reality, school spirit takes a horrifying literal turn, and the countdown to Fear Day is everything.

---
