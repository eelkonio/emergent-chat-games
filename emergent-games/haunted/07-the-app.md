# The App

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Digital Supernatural  
**Description:** "Whisper" launched last week. New social media. Anonymous posts, local feed, addictive UI. Everyone at school is on it. But users are changing — blank stares, repetitive speech, checking their phones every 30 seconds even when nothing's there. The app doesn't exist on any app store. Nobody can remember who told them about it.  
**Intent:** Explore digital possession — where the supernatural adapts to teen social media behavior, and the line between addiction and possession is terrifyingly thin.

---
