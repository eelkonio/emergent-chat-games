# The Dare

**Genre:** Teen Horror / Urban Legend  
**Description:** "Say her name three times in the bathroom mirror. Bloody Mary." Everyone knows the dare. It's stupid. It's for kids. But Olivia did it at Marcus's party and she hasn't come to school since. Her parents say she's "visiting family." Her Snap location says she's still in that bathroom.  
**Intent:** Explore the activation of myth — when a universal childhood ritual actually works, and the consequences spread from the one who did it to the ones who know about it.

---

## Prompt

