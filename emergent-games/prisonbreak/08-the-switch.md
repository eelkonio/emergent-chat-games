# The Switch

**Genre:** Prison Escape / Identity Thriller  
**Description:** Inmate #4471, Daniel Reeves, is being released next Tuesday. You've been watching him for months. Same height, same build, close enough in face — especially after you lost weight and grew your hair. The processing guard on Tuesdays is new. The ID photos in the system are 3 years old. If you can get Reeves out of the way, take his place in processing, answer his questions, sign his forms, and walk out the front door — you're free. One problem: Reeves doesn't want to cooperate. Why would he? He's going home.  
**Intent:** Explore identity theft under extreme pressure, the moral cost of stealing another person's freedom timeline, and the art of becoming someone else entirely.
