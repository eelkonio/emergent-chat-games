# Meeting the Parents

**Genre:** Dating / Cultural Drama  
**Description:** 6 months together. It's time. But "meeting the parents" means something different when your cultures clash on religion, class, profession, or the basic definition of what a good partner looks like. You're not just meeting people — you're meeting a worldview.  
**Intent:** Explore cultural navigation in romance, the tension between partner and family loyalty, and how love exists within systems of expectation.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions WITHIN the family meeting scenario. Partner, their family, and cultural dynamics act independently. No script — everything from system state. Write with specificity — the food, the silence after a wrong comment, the translation that loses something.

Loop: State → family dynamics → cultural friction → partner's position → event → player choice → cascade → new state.
```
