# The Poly-Crisis

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Cascade Convergence  
**Description:** They were never separate problems. The pandemic emptied the offices, the recession emptied the savings, the drought emptied the fields, the AI emptied the jobs, and the migration filled the borders — and each one made every other one worse, all at once, faster than anyone could triage. This is the poly-crisis: not a single apocalypse but every apocalypse braided together, where the fix for one is poison for another. Close the borders to stop the outbreak and you strand the harvest labor and starve the cities. Print money to stop the depression and you kill the currency. Every lever you pull yanks three others you didn't touch. From above, you steer the whole tangled machine of civilization as its systems fail in chorus, deciding which crisis you let burn so you can fight another, and whether an interconnected world can survive being interconnected. This is the final exam of the apocalypse genre: everything, at once.  
**Intent:** Explore the convergence of every civilizational threat into a single interlocking cascade, the deadly trade-offs where solving one crisis worsens another, and whether tightly-coupled global systems can survive shocks that hit them all together.
