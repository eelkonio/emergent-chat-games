# The Great Deleveraging

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Economic Collapse  
**Description:** The debt finally came due. Decades of borrowing against the future ran into a wall of rising rates, aging populations, and a productivity shock nobody priced in. A mid-sized nation defaults on a Tuesday; by Friday the contagion is in the core. Pension funds are underwater, a reserve currency is wobbling, and half the world's "assets" turn out to be claims on a growth that isn't coming. This isn't a recession — it's a repricing of civilization's balance sheet. From above, you move central banks, sovereign funds, and the influencers who move herds of retail money, deciding which institutions you save, which you let fail, and which lie you let stand for one more quarter. Money is a shared story. You are steering the moment the story breaks.  
**Intent:** Explore systemic financial collapse as a crisis of collective belief, the impossibility of saving everyone in a deleveraging, and the political fury that arrives when the promise of endless growth is finally called.
