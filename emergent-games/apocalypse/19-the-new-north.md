# The New North

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Winners and Losers  
**Description:** Climate change doesn't end the world evenly. As the equator burns and the coasts drown, a band of the far north — Canada, Scandinavia, Russia, the newly-thawed steppe — is becoming the most valuable real estate on the planet: arable, temperate, watered, and suddenly the destination for everyone and everything with somewhere better to be. The map of power is being redrawn by latitude. The old great powers of the temperate middle are cracking as their breadbaskets dry, while yesterday's frozen backwaters inherit the century. From above, you steer the rise of the new north and the fall of the old center, deciding which nations you let ascend, which you let collapse gracefully, and whether the greatest transfer of wealth and power in history happens through negotiation or through war. The apocalypse is also a coronation. Somebody wins the warming world. You decide who — and what they owe the losers.  
**Intent:** Explore climate change as a redistribution of geographic power rather than a uniform catastrophe, the rise of the far north and fall of the temperate center, and whether a civilizational reshuffling can happen without war.
