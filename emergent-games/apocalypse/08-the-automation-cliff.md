# The Automation Cliff

**Genre:** Apocalypse / AI Unemployment & Recession  
**Description:** Two curses arrived at once. The AI systems that were supposed to make everyone rich started doing the jobs instead — not slowly, but in a single brutal year, across law, logistics, coding, radiology, customer service. And the recession that everyone was warned about hit the same quarter, so there are no new jobs to catch the displaced. Forty percent unemployment among white-collar professionals in eighteen months, in a system that assumed 5%. The profits are astronomical and pooling in a few hands; the streets are filling. From above, you steer the corporations deploying the AI, the governments that must feed the newly useless, and the demagogues harvesting the rage, deciding whether the productivity windfall becomes shared abundance or a fortress guarded by drones. The machines can do the work. The question is what the people are now for.  
**Intent:** Explore the collision of mass AI displacement with economic contraction, the failure of "retraining" against exponential capability, and the political fury of a professional class made suddenly worthless.
