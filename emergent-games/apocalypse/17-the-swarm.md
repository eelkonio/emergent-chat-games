# The Swarm

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Autonomous Weapons Proliferation  
**Description:** The weapon of mass destruction now costs less than a car and ships in a backpack. A thousand palm-sized drones, off-the-shelf parts, an open-source AI that handles targeting, coordination, and facial recognition — a swarm that can find and kill a specific person, or everyone in a stadium, with no human pilot and no way to jam all of it. Every angry group, every cornered regime, every lone fanatic now has access to what used to require an air force. The first swarm attack just hit a summit; the second is being built in a garage somewhere. Deterrence assumed you could find and punish the attacker — but a swarm has no return address and needs no army. From above, you steer the powers scrambling to ban, control, or build the swarms, deciding whether autonomous slaughter can be put back in the box, or whether the age of the individual with the power of a state has simply begun.  
**Intent:** Explore cheap autonomous weapons as the democratization of mass killing, the collapse of deterrence against attackers who can't be found, and the terror of a world where anyone can command a lethal swarm.
