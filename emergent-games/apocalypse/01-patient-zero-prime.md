# Patient Zero Prime

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Pandemic Geopolitics  
**Description:** A respiratory pathogen with a two-week silent incubation and a 6% case fatality rate surfaces in three cities on three continents in the same week — a pattern too clean to be natural. Genomic sequencing shows engineered signatures nobody will admit to. Borders are already closing. Markets are already falling. Vaccine platforms exist but manufacturing is nationalized within days. From above, you don't fight the virus with a mask — you move nations, labs, and money, deciding who shares data and who hoards it, who locks down and who denies, which leader you elevate and which you let fall. You are the hidden hand steering a fracturing world through the first hours of the next pandemic — the one we said we were ready for.  
**Intent:** Explore pandemic response as a geopolitical power game, the collapse of the honesty that public health requires, and the terrifying gap between what science can do and what sovereignty will allow.
