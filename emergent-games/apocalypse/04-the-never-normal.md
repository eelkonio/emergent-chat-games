# The Never Normal

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Societal Acceleration  
**Description:** Nothing has ended. That's the problem. The virus didn't kill everyone, the economy didn't fully collapse, the climate didn't finish, the AI didn't take over — but all of it is happening at once, and it never stops changing long enough for anyone to adjust. Every institution — schools, courts, churches, markets, families — was built assuming that tomorrow resembles today. That assumption is dead. Jobs vanish faster than people can retrain; laws are obsolete before they pass; a truth on Monday is a lie by Friday. Society isn't collapsing from any single blow. It's dissolving from vertigo. From above, you steer nations and institutions through permanent whiplash, deciding which parts of society you let break so others can bend. The apocalypse isn't an event. It's a speed.  
**Intent:** Explore the thesis that the real catastrophe is the rate of change itself — that human institutions and psychology have adaptation limits, and that outrunning them is its own kind of end.
