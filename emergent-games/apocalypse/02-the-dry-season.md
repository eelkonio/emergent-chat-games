# The Dry Season

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Climate Migration Geopolitics  
**Description:** The monsoon failed for the third year running. A belt of the planet — from the Sahel through South Asia — is becoming physically uninhabitable during the summer wet-bulb months: temperatures at which a healthy human in the shade dies in six hours. A hundred million people are on the move, and the borders they're moving toward are electrifying their fences. Rich nations debate "climate visas" while building walls. Poor nations empty out. From above, you steer the great reshuffling: which countries you push toward absorption and which toward the gun, which corporation you convince to build in the new north, which leader you elevate to sell the unsellable. You are not stopping the heat. You are deciding who survives it, and who is left in the dry season.  
**Intent:** Explore mass climate displacement as the defining geopolitical force of the century, the moral collapse at hardening borders, and whether the map of humanity can be redrawn without catastrophe.
