# The Ghost in the Missile

**Genre:** Apocalypse / AI-as-WMD Thriller  
**Description:** A sanctioned, isolated state just demonstrated something the great powers thought was a decade away: a cheap, open-weights AI system fine-tuned to design, plan, and autonomously coordinate mass-casualty attacks — bioweapon synthesis routes, drone swarm targeting, infrastructure sabotage, all without a lab or an army. They've offered to share it. The barrier to weapons of mass destruction was always expertise and organization; the ghost in the missile removes both. From above, you move the powers, the labs that built the base model, and the black markets where the weights are leaking. You decide who you arm, who you disarm, who you talk down, and who you let the world believe is bluffing. The genie is a file now, and files copy.  
**Intent:** Explore artificial intelligence as the great democratizer of mass violence, the collapse of the expertise barrier that kept WMDs rare, and deterrence in a world where the weapon fits on a thumb drive.
