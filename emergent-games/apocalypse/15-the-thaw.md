# The Thaw

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Permafrost Feedback  
**Description:** The ground in the far north is melting, and it is exhaling. Decades of warming have crossed the threshold where the permafrost — a frozen vault holding twice the carbon of the atmosphere and pathogens no living immune system has ever met — begins to release on its own, warming the planet, which melts more permafrost, which releases more. The feedback loop has started, and it does not need us anymore. Out of the thawing ground come two horrors at once: a carbon burp that could blow past every emissions target overnight, and an anthrax outbreak from a reindeer carcass frozen since before antibiotics — the first of many revenants. From above, you steer the Arctic nations racing to drill the newly-open ground, the scientists screaming about the loop, and the world deciding whether to attempt the unthinkable: geoengineering the sky. The Earth has taken the wheel. You are trying to grab it back.  
**Intent:** Explore runaway climate feedback as the moment human agency slips, ancient pathogens released from the ice, and the desperate gamble of planetary geoengineering with no way to test it first.
