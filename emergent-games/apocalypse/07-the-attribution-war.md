# The Attribution War

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Bioweapon Brinkmanship  
**Description:** The pandemic is six weeks old and four million are dead. The sequencing is unambiguous: this was engineered. What's ambiguous is by whom. Three nations have the capability, two have motive, and every intelligence agency is producing the attribution its government wants to hear. One power is already massing forces on a border. Another is demanding retaliation "with equivalent means." The truth exists — there really is a patient zero, a lab, a decision — but truth arrives slowly and armies move fast. From above, you steer the accusers and the accused, deciding which evidence surfaces, which gets buried, whom you let take the blame, and whether the response to a biological attack becomes a nuclear one. The virus was the first weapon. The second is certainty.  
**Intent:** Explore the deadly gap between a bioweapon attack and provable attribution, how intelligence bends to political need, and the escalation logic that can turn a plague into a world war.
