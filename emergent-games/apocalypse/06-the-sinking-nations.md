# The Sinking Nations

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Sovereignty & Sea-Level Rise  
**Description:** A nation is about to become the first in modern history to physically cease to exist — not conquered, not merged, but drowned. Its islands will be uninhabitable within fifteen years and gone within fifty. Its people have citizenship, culture, a UN seat, and nowhere to stand. So they're doing the unthinkable: buying land inside another sovereign country, negotiating to move an entire nation, government and all, onto foreign soil while keeping their flag. Others will follow — delta states, coastal megacities. From above, you broker the relocation of countries: who sells land to whom, whether a nation can exist without territory, which host absorbs a people and which turns them into stateless ghosts. The map has always been permanent. You are steering the moment it stops being.  
**Intent:** Explore the unprecedented legal and human question of relocating an entire sovereign nation, what a country is when its land is gone, and the birth of a new kind of statelessness at civilizational scale.
