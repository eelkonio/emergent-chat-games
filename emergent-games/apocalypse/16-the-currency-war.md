# The Currency War

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Monetary Collapse  
**Description:** The dollar is dying, and everyone is fighting over what replaces it. Eighty years of the world running on one reserve currency is ending — killed by unpayable debt, weaponized sanctions that scared every rival into building an exit, and a wave of AI-driven, algorithmic capital that moves trillions in milliseconds. Now the contenders are lunging: a digital yuan backed by Chinese trade, a resurgent gold bloc, a stateless crypto-reserve, a basket the IMF is desperately assembling. Currencies are the plumbing of civilization, and the plumbing is being ripped out mid-flood. From above, you steer the central banks, the sovereign funds, the crypto whales, and the influencers who can trigger a run with a post, deciding which money the world will trust next — and whether the transition is an orderly handover or the financial equivalent of a world war. When money dies, everything priced in it dies too.  
**Intent:** Explore the collapse of the reserve-currency order, the scramble to define the next global money, and the systemic chaos of rebuilding civilization's plumbing while the water is still on.
