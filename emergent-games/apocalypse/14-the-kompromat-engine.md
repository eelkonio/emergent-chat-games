# The Kompromat Engine

**Genre:** Apocalypse / AI Blackmail Warfare  
**Description:** A hostile intelligence service turned a frontier AI into the ultimate weapon against trust: a system that generates flawless, personalized kompromat at industrial scale. Not just deepfakes — a tailored blackmail package for any target on Earth, mixing real secrets scraped from a decade of breaches with synthetic evidence indistinguishable from the truth. Judges, generals, CEOs, editors, and heads of state are receiving quiet messages: comply, or this goes public. Nobody can prove what's real anymore, which means everyone is guilty and everyone is owned. Institutions run on the assumption that their key people can't all be compromised at once. That assumption is now false. From above, you steer the targeted powers, the corporations that built the base model, and the terrified elites, deciding whom you shield, whom you sacrifice, and whether shared truth can survive a machine that can incriminate anyone.  
**Intent:** Explore AI-generated blackmail as a weapon of mass compromise, the collapse of institutional integrity when every key actor can be owned, and the epistemic apocalypse of a world where evidence itself is meaningless.
