# The Vaccine Cold War

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Pandemic Nationalism  
**Description:** The cure exists. That's what makes it a war. Six months into a pandemic killing millions, three rival blocs have each cracked a working vaccine — and each is using it as a weapon of statecraft. Doses go to allies, are withheld from rivals, and are dangled in front of the undecided in exchange for bases, votes, and loyalty. The patents are locked; the manufacturing is nationalized; a "people's vaccine" movement is being crushed by the very companies that could end the plague tomorrow. Meanwhile the virus, indifferent to borders, keeps mutating in the unvaccinated billions, threatening to escape every vaccine at once. From above, you steer the blocs, the pharma giants, and the movements, deciding whether the cure becomes a common inheritance or the sharpest weapon in a new cold war. The plague could end this year. Politics is why it won't.  
**Intent:** Explore vaccine nationalism as great-power warfare, the moral catastrophe of a hoarded cure, and the epidemiological truth that a pandemic unsolved anywhere is unsolved everywhere.
