# The Hundred Million

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Mega-Exodus  
**Description:** It is the largest movement of human beings in history, and it is happening now. A hundred million people from a single drowning delta nation are leaving — not fleeing a war, not chasing a dream, but walking away from land that will be under the sea within their children's lifetimes. Every neighbor is closing its borders. Every distant nation is debating quotas measured in thousands while the exodus is measured in tens of millions. There is no war to stop, no aid drop that helps, no wall long enough. From above, you steer the sending nation, the transit states, the reluctant destinations, and the voices screaming for and against the newcomers, deciding whether a hundred million people become citizens somewhere or ghosts everywhere. This is what the climate century actually looks like: not fire and flood on the news, but an ocean of human beings with nowhere to go.  
**Intent:** Explore the largest forced migration in human history as a management problem with no clean solution, the hardening of the world's borders against the drowning, and whether humanity can absorb its own displacement without fracturing.
