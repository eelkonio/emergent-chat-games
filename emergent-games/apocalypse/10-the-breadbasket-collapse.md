# The Breadbasket Collapse

**Genre:** Apocalypse / Global Famine Geopolitics  
**Description:** Three of the world's four great grain regions failed in the same year — a synchronized drought-and-flood one-two that the climate models called a tail risk and reality just made the base case. Global grain reserves are at seventy days and falling. The exporting nations are slamming their ports shut to feed their own; the importing nations, many already destabilized by heat and debt, are staring at famine for a billion people. Food is now the ultimate strategic weapon, and everyone has just realized it at once. From above, you steer the grain giants, the fertilizer cartels, the commodity traders, and the hungry, deciding who eats, who starves, whose government falls, and whether the century's first true famine-war stays regional or goes global. Bread was always politics. Now it's everything.  
**Intent:** Explore synchronized crop failure as a civilizational threat, food as the most fundamental weapon, and the brutal triage of a world that suddenly cannot feed itself.
