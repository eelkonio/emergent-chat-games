# The Refugees

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** Thirty-seven people in your cargo hold. They paid everything they had — which wasn't much. Your hold isn't designed for humans. The air recyclers will fail in six days. The destination is eight days away. And the patrol behind you isn't looking for contraband — they're looking for these people specifically.  
**Intent:** Explore the morality of refugee transport, how human cargo changes every calculation, and the impossible math of survival when the numbers don't work.

---
