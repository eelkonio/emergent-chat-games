# The Cargo

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** Rule number one of hauling: don't open sealed cargo. You opened it. You shouldn't have. Now you know what you're carrying and you can't unknow it. The thing in Hold Three is alive. Or was alive. Or is becoming alive. And everyone on this route — pirates, corps, governments — would kill to possess it. Including, possibly, the people who hired you.  
**Intent:** Explore Pandora's box in a shipping container, the responsibility that comes with knowledge, and how a simple cargo job becomes the most important decision of your life.

---

## Prompt

