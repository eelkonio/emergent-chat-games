# The War

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** The Meridian Alliance and the Free Worlds are at war — properly this time. Not cold-war posturing. Real fleets, real casualties, real borders closing. You're a neutral trader caught in the middle. Both sides want your cargo capacity. Both sides threaten neutrals who trade with the other. Neutrality is becoming impossible — and choosing a side changes everything.  
**Intent:** Explore neutrality in wartime, how conflict forces binary choices on people who live in gray areas, and the trader's impossible position when the lanes become battlegrounds.

---
