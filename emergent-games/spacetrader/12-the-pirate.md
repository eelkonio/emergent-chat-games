# The Pirate

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** Your ship is armed. You know the lanes. You know when traders are heavy-loaded and light on escort. It would be so easy. One job — hit a Consolidated Freight automated hauler, take the cargo, sell it at a black market port. Victimless, almost. But you know it's not one job. It never is. The line between trader and pirate is a line you cross once.  
**Intent:** Explore the economic pressure that creates piracy, whether the first step is really the point of no return, and how moral identity shifts when survival demands it.

---

## Prompt

