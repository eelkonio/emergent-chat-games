# The Alien Artifact

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** It was in a crate marked "geological samples." You bought the crate at a salvage auction for 200 credits. Inside: a device that is clearly not human-made. It responds to touch. It emits energy patterns your instruments can't classify. And within 48 hours of your purchase, three separate parties contacted you — offering prices that make no sense. Unless they know what it is. And you don't.  
**Intent:** Explore first contact through commerce, how alien technology disrupts human power structures, and the trader's dilemma when their cargo might be worth more than money.

---
