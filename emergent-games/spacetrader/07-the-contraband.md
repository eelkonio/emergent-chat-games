# The Contraband

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** New regs. Cargo scanners at every gate. What used to be gray-market is now red-listed. Your hold is full of perfectly legal medical stims that became illegal three days ago when the Alliance reclassified them. You're 400,000 credits in product with nowhere legal to sell it — and the new enforcement patrols are hungry for their first big bust.  
**Intent:** Explore how legal boundaries are arbitrary in frontier space, the difference between smuggling and commerce under changing law, and the trader's adaptation to hostile regulation.

---
