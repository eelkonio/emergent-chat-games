# The Crew

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** Your old crew scattered after the Kepler incident. You've got a ship and a job but no one to fly with. Frontier stations are full of people looking for berths — every one of them running from something. You need a pilot, an engineer, a medic, and a gunner. You have one week before the job window closes. Choose wrong and you die in the black. Choose right and you might have found family.  
**Intent:** Explore crew assembly as found-family origin, how trust is built under pressure, and the gamble of inviting strangers into your survival.

---

## Prompt
