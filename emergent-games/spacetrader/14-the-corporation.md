# The Corporation

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** GenDyne Logistics wants to buy your ship. Not the physical ship — your routes, your contacts, your reputation. They're offering 400,000 credits and a salaried position. It's more money than you'll earn in five years of independent hauling. Or you can refuse — and watch as they undercut every contract you have until you sell for 100,000. They're corporatizing the frontier. And you're in the way.  
**Intent:** Explore independent enterprise vs. corporate absorption, what we lose when efficiency replaces personality, and the frontier's dying independent spirit.

---
