# The Wrongful Conviction

**Genre:** True Crime / Justice Drama  
**Description:** Marcus Reed has been in prison for 15 years for a murder he says he didn't commit. The Innocence Project passed on his case — "not enough new evidence." His mother contacted you. The deeper you dig, the more the conviction unravels: coerced witness, lost DNA evidence, a detective with a pattern of forcing confessions. But proving innocence isn't enough — you need to prove it on the record. And the DA who convicted him is now a judge.  
**Intent:** Explore systemic injustice, the difficulty of overturning convictions even with new evidence, and whether public pressure through media can break what legal channels wouldn't.

---
