# The Cult

**Genre:** True Crime / Cult Investigation  
**Description:** The Garden of Light. A wellness commune in rural Oregon. Seven deaths in 5 years — all ruled suicide or natural causes. The families say otherwise. The members say they're at peace. The leader says you're "projecting fear." Your podcast season starts with an interview with a former member — but they're still afraid. The commune is still operating. People are still inside.  
**Intent:** Explore coercive control organizations, the difficulty of proving harm when victims deny it, and whether exposing a cult helps or endangers its members.

---
