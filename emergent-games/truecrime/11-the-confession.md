# The Confession

**Genre:** True Crime / False Confession Investigation  
**Description:** A man confessed to murder. He gave details. He signed the statement. He went to prison. But: he was interrogated for 19 hours straight. He has an IQ of 72. The "details" he gave were all things published in the local paper. And the actual forensic evidence doesn't match his confession. Someone confessed to a crime they didn't commit — and the system doesn't want to hear it.  
**Intent:** Explore coerced confessions, cognitive vulnerability in interrogation, and the system's investment in finality over truth.

---
