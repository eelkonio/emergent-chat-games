# The Copycat

**Genre:** True Crime / Moral Horror  
**Description:** Your podcast about an unsolved 2019 murder just inspired a copycat crime. Same method. Same victim profile. A note at the scene: "Episode 7 showed me how." You're responsible. Not legally (probably). But morally? The details you broadcast — the specific method, the location type, the victim selection — became an instruction manual. Someone listened, learned, and acted. And they're attributing it to you.  
**Intent:** Explore the responsibility of true crime media, whether detailed reporting enables violence, and how to handle guilt for something you didn't do but might have caused.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player's podcast inspired a copycat crime. Moral responsibility, legal exposure, and the case itself all demand attention simultaneously. No script — everything from system state.

Loop: State → copycat evidence → guilt/responsibility → police pressure → media attention → player choice → respond/investigate/process → new state.

SETTING: Season 4 covered the 2019 murder of Jessica Hawthorn — strangled with a specific ligature, body left in a specific position, in a parking garage. Your Episode 7 detailed the method (forensic accuracy for the case). Three days ago: a new victim found, same method, same positioning, same type of location. A printed note: "Thank you for Episode 7. Now I understand." Police are at your door. Media is calling you "the podcaster whose show killed someone." Your audience is in freefall. And the copycat is still out there — and still listening to your show.

TURN: 1 day.

METERS (0-100, start 50): MORAL STANDING · LEGAL EXPOSURE · AUDIENCE TRUST · POLICE COOPERATION · MENTAL HEALTH↓ · COPYCAT INVESTIGATION · PUBLIC PERCEPTION
MENTAL HEALTH drops as guilt accumulates. Your responsibility is legally grey but morally vivid.

AGENTS:
- Detective Walsh (investigating copycat, needs your cooperation, blames you subtly)

- Your lawyer (first amendment defense, but "moral responsibility" isn't a legal term)

CONSISTENCY RULE: Ensure correct increasing turn counts.
```
