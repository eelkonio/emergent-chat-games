# The Cop

**Genre:** True Crime / Police Corruption  
**Description:** What if the detective who investigated the murder... committed it? Detective Frank Harris worked the Elaine Cho case in 2016. He found "no viable leads." Case went cold. But his DNA was at a location he claimed he never visited. His alibi depends on a partner who's now dead. And 3 other women matching Cho's profile went missing in his patrol area between 2014-2019. You're investigating the investigator. And he still has his badge.  
**Intent:** Explore the terror of corrupt law enforcement, investigating someone who knows investigation, and the system that protects its own at any cost.

---
