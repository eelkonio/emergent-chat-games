# The Pattern

**Genre:** True Crime / Decades-Long Mystery  
**Description:** Seemingly unrelated cases across 30 years. A drowned teenager in 1994. A missing hitchhiker in 2001. A "suicide" in 2009. An unsolved murder in 2017. Different cities, different investigators, different victims. But you found the thread: all happened within 50 miles of Interstate 78. All victims were passing through, not local. All were alone. Something lives along that highway. And nobody's been looking at the geography.  
**Intent:** Explore geographic profiling, the horror of undetected serial crime hidden by jurisdictional fragmentation, and connecting dots across decades.

---
