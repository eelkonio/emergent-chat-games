# The Celebrity

**Genre:** True Crime / Celebrity Investigation  
**Description:** Rock star Jax Morrison died in his hotel room at 44. Ruled "accidental overdose." His fans don't believe it: wrong drugs (he'd been clean 2 years), wrong hotel (he always stayed elsewhere), wrong people (his manager was present but called 911 "too late"). The estate is worth $200M. The manager inherits management rights. Your podcast episode about the inconsistencies got 2M downloads. Now the manager's lawyers are calling. And Jax's ex-wife says she has recordings.  
**Intent:** Explore celebrity death investigations where money corrupts evidence, fan obsession can be weaponized, and powerful estates have every reason to maintain the official narrative.

---
