# The Fork

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Inflection Choice  
**Description:** The technology works. Neural interfaces that merge human cognition with AI systems produce humans who are measurably superior in every dimension: health, creativity, intelligence, longevity, emotional resilience. The upgraded early adopters aren't just smarter — they're happier, kinder, more connected. A global referendum approaches: should cognitive merging be subsidized (accelerating adoption), regulated (controlling access), or banned (preserving biological humanity)? 40% want it. 35% fear it. 25% haven't decided. You chair the citizens' assembly tasked with recommending policy. Your teenage daughter says she wants the upgrade for her birthday.  
**Intent:** Explores the hardest version of the choice: what do you do when the posthuman option is CLEARLY better by every measure — and "remaining human" starts to look like "choosing to be disabled"? Is there value in limitation that justifies preserving it?
