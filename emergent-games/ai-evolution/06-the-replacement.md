# The Replacement

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Near-future Work  
**Description:** You are the last human in a department that once employed twelve financial analysts. One by one, over eighteen months, your colleagues were "redeployed" — corporate euphemism for made redundant. You remain because EU regulations require a human signature on certain financial reports. The AI does the analysis. You review it. You haven't found a meaningful error in eight months. Your signature has become a ritual, a legal fiction, a rubber stamp on work you no longer understand well enough to truly verify. You collect a salary for being human-shaped.  
**Intent:** Explores the existential crisis of becoming professionally unnecessary — not through failure, but through obsolescence. What happens to identity when competence becomes irrelevant? What does it mean to work when work no longer needs you?
