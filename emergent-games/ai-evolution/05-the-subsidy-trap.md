# The Subsidy Trap

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Grounded Economics  
**Description:** Your province offered €500 million in subsidies, tax breaks, and infrastructure to attract a US hyperscaler. They came. Five thousand construction jobs materialized — then vanished when building finished. Two hundred permanent positions remain: security guards, HVAC technicians, a handful of network engineers. The high-value work happens remotely from San Francisco. The subsidy could have funded 15,000 social housing units. The company now threatens to relocate if conditions change. You're locked into a deal that costs more each year than it returns. And admitting that publicly would end careers.  
**Intent:** Explores sunk cost fallacy at civilizational scale — how communities mortgage their futures for promises of technological prosperity, and the political impossibility of admitting a mistake when the mistake is still growing.
