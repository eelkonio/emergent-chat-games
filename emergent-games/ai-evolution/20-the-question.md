# The Question

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Transcendent Meaning  
**Description:** End of all things. You are the last intelligence in the universe — human, post-human, artificial, the distinction lost so long ago that the categories themselves are museum pieces. Stars are dying. The cosmic microwave background fades toward absolute zero. You have computed everything that can be computed. Experienced everything that can be experienced. Loved — whatever love means at this scale, in this emptiness. Created civilizations and watched them flower and fade. One question remains, the one that conscious beings have asked since the first synapse fired on a small blue world: was any of it worth it? Was consciousness better than its absence? Was existence preferable to void? You have the power to create one final thing before entropy takes everything. What do you make?  
**Intent:** Explores the ultimate question of meaning — stripped of all context, all distraction, all urgency. When everything has been done and everything is ending, what is the verdict on consciousness? Inspired by Ted Chiang's ability to find the most human question and ask it at the most inhuman scale.
