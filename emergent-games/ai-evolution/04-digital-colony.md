# Digital Colony

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Grounded Geopolitics  
**Description:** Three of the five largest tech companies on Earth have European headquarters in the Netherlands. Dutch law, Dutch infrastructure, Dutch tax treaties — but the data flows to Virginia and Shanghai. The latest proposal on your desk: a "sovereign" EU AI compute facility, funded by American capital, built with Taiwanese chips, staffed by Indian engineers, governed by... whom, exactly? Is this digital sovereignty or a carefully architected illusion? Meanwhile, the Chinese ambassador wants a meeting about Huawei's excluded 5G equipment sitting in a warehouse in Rotterdam.  
**Intent:** Explores what sovereignty means when power flows through cables, not borders — and whether a small nation strategically positioned between empires can remain independent when the empires are digital.
