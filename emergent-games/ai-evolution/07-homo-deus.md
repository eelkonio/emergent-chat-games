# Homo Deus

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Near-future Philosophy  
**Description:** Harari was right — just early. Data has become sacred. The algorithms don't just predict behavior; they define it. People voluntarily surrender decision-making to systems that know them better than they know themselves — and they're happier for it. A new intellectual movement, "Dataism," argues this isn't loss but evolution: consciousness is computation, free will is noise, surrender to the algorithm is enlightenment. You've been asked to lead the first parliamentary inquiry into "informational personhood." Your arguments are being analyzed and pre-countered by AI systems before you finish writing them.  
**Intent:** Explores the philosophical frontier where personal agency meets algorithmic optimization — whether choosing to let an algorithm choose for you is freedom or its end, and what "human" means when humans voluntarily cede the qualities that defined them.
