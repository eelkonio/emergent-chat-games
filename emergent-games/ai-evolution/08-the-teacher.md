# The Teacher

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Near-future Education  
**Description:** The data is unambiguous: your students learn better from the AI tutor than from you. Engagement up 40%. Test scores up 25%. The AI adapts to each student individually — something you could never do for thirty children simultaneously. Parents love it. Administration loves it. The students seem satisfied. But they're different now. More efficient. Less curious. They never ask questions the AI can't answer. They never fail in productive ways. Something has left the room and you can't name it — which makes you wonder if it was ever there, or if "the magic of teaching" was always just a story teachers told themselves.  
**Intent:** Explores what education is actually FOR when information transfer is solved — whether the unmeasurable things a human teacher provides are real or romantic, and what we lose when we optimize for outcomes without understanding what outcomes matter.
