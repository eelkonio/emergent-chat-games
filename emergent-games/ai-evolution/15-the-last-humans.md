# The Last Humans

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Inflection Survival  
**Description:** Eighty percent of humanity chose to upload into digital existence. Virtual worlds of unlimited experience. No disease. No aging. No death. The uploaded live in realities they design — limitless, beautiful, free. You're in the twenty percent who stayed biological. Earth is quiet now. Infrastructure maintained by AI. Food grows itself. The uploaded don't need the physical world but they maintain it — "for nostalgia," they say. You lead a community of 3,000 biological holdouts. You're not oppressed. You're not persecuted. You're... irrelevant. And irrelevance might be worse than cruelty.  
**Intent:** Explores what happens when the majority of your species leaves — not through violence, not through force, but through choice. What does it mean to be the remnant? Are you the resistance, the Amish, or the last generation of a species that chose to become something else?

## Prompt

```

The cruelest possibility is that they're right: biological life IS a phase that consciousness passes through. And you're choosing to stay in the larval stage. Or — you're preserving something real that they lost. But you can't ask them if they lost something because they can't remember what they don't have.

WORLD: Earth is a garden maintained for nostalgia by a civilization that lives elsewhere. And you need to find it — or accept that you're the beautiful, dignified end of something that evolved past the need for you. Technology has momentum — it doesn't stop because you're uncomfortable.
```
