# Emergent Games — AI Evolution
<img src="logo.png" width=200 align="right">

Twenty games exploring AI not as a tool to govern, but as an evolutionary force reshaping civilization. From a Dutch village fighting a data center to a post-human intelligence asking whether its creators still matter.

Inspired by Kevin Kelly, Yuval Harari, Eliezer Yudkowsky, Iain M. Banks, Greg Egan, Ted Chiang, and the daily reality of communities competing with server farms for water and electricity.

The spectrum:
- **Games 01-05:** Grounded. The Netherlands. Data centers, energy grids, local politics, global economics.
- **Games 06-10:** Near-future. AI reshaping work, meaning, relationships, truth.
- **Games 11-15:** Inflection. AI breaking boundaries, alignment failing, technology wanting.
- **Games 16-20:** Transcendent. Post-human, civilizational, what comes after us.
