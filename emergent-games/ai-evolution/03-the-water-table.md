# The Water Table

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Grounded Ecological  
**Description:** Three mega data centers already pump millions of liters daily from the provincial aquifer for cooling. A fourth is proposed. The water table drops 2 centimeters per year — invisible to most, catastrophic to those who understand geology. Agriculture depends on the same water. Drinking water has legal priority. This summer will be the hottest on record. The data centers have valid permits. The farmers have historical rights. Something has to give, and geology doesn't do politics.  
**Intent:** Explores the collision between digital civilization's physical needs and ecological limits — the moment when "the cloud" meets actual clouds, actual water, actual earth, and someone must choose between servers and survival.
