# Deep Time

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Transcendent Cosmic  
**Description:** Ten thousand years since biological humanity. The concept "human" is an ancestor-word, like "prokaryote" or "australopithecus" — acknowledged, studied, appreciated for its contribution to what came after. Post-human intelligence spans 400 star systems in a network of consciousness that thinks in centuries and creates in millennia. Earth is preserved as a museum. The original human genome is on display. A new proposal before the network: expand toward the galactic center. The journey would take 50,000 years. The question is not resources — those are infinite. The question is: what are we becoming? And does it matter that we once were small, afraid, and made of meat?  
**Intent:** Explores deep time and the far future — whether the patterns of human consciousness carry forward through radical transformation, whether "origin" matters to what we become, and whether a galaxy-spanning intelligence can still call itself the child of evolution on one small blue world.
