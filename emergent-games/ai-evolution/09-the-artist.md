# The Artist

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Near-future Creativity  
**Description:** Twenty years of practice. Thousands of hours learning color theory, composition, the way light falls on skin. Your hands know things your mind can't articulate. And now a system trained on billions of images — including yours — generates work in your style in twelve seconds. Galleries are showing AI art. Collectors buy it: cheaper, unlimited, no difficult temperament attached. Critics say "art is about the human experience." But people cry at AI-generated images. They feel seen by them. They hang them in their homes. If the response is real, does the source matter? And if it doesn't — what were those twenty years for?  
**Intent:** Explores the deepest question of human creativity — whether art derives meaning from the process of making, the experience of receiving, or something else entirely. And what happens to a life built on a skill that has become abundant.
