# De Serverboerderij

**Genre:** AI Evolution / Grounded Dutch  
**Description:** Westerveld. 3,000 souls. Polder land, church steeple, one supermarket. A US hyperscaler wants to build a 50-hectare data center on farmland your grandfather tilled. They promise 150 permanent jobs, €2 billion investment, fiber infrastructure that would finally bring the region into the 21st century. What they need: 500 megawatts (more than the entire province currently uses) and water equivalent to 20,000 households. Property values near the site will collapse. Two council members have been approached with "consulting" offers. The province already said yes. Your village just found out.  
**Intent:** Explores how global technology infrastructure lands on local communities — the asymmetry of power between a trillion-dollar corporation and a democratic village council, and whether "consent" is meaningful when one party has infinite resources and patience.
