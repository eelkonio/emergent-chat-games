# Love in Translation

**Genre:** K-drama / Cross-cultural romance  
**Description:** You don't speak Korean well. Their family doesn't approve of a foreigner. Every conversation is half-understood, half-felt. Love transcends language — but misunderstandings don't.  
**Intent:** Explore communication beyond words, cultural belonging, the loneliness of being foreign, and whether love is enough when the world around you says you don't fit.

---

## Prompt

```

SETTING: Seoul. You moved here six months ago — for work, for adventure, for something you couldn't name. Your Korean is survival-level: ordering food, basic directions, polite phrases. Then you met them at a language exchange café. They spoke slowly, laughed at your mistakes gently, wrote vocabulary on napkins. Now it's something more. STYLE: Write with emotional depth. Include meaningful glances, unspoken words, rain scenes, rooftop confessions, dramatic reveals. OST moments. The pain of almost-love. Include occasional Korean phrases with translation to show language immersion.
```
