# CEO Undercover

**Genre:** K-drama / Identity romance  
**Description:** You're the chaebol heir, working entry-level at your own company to understand the people. Nobody knows. You fall for a colleague who treats you like an equal — because they think you are one. If they find out, everything changes.  
**Intent:** Explore authenticity, class privilege, the fear of being loved for what you have vs. who you are, and the moral weight of a well-intentioned deception.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions WITHIN the world. Other characters act independently with their own feelings, secrets, and agendas. No script — everything from system state and character emotions.

You're working in the marketing department under a false name, wearing off-brand clothes, taking the subway, eating at the company cafeteria. Your father's test: understand the workers before you lead them. You weren't supposed to care about anyone. But the colleague at the next desk — who shares their lunch, who fights for fair overtime, who looks at you like you matter as a person — is making this impossible. Every day the lie gets heavier. Every day the connection gets realer.

TURN: 1 week

CONSISTENCY RULE: Ensure correct increasing turn counts.

START: Create characters with hidden emotional profiles, begin Turn 1.
```
