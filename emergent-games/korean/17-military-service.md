# Military Service

**Genre:** K-drama / Long-distance romance  
**Description:** Eighteen months apart. Rare letters, rarer calls. They're growing without you. You're growing without them. Will you still fit together when the uniform comes off?  
**Intent:** Explore the test of absence, personal growth during separation, trust without proximity, and whether love can survive when both people become someone new.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions WITHIN the world. Other characters act independently with their own feelings, secrets, and agendas. No script — everything from system state and character emotions.

New friends, new routines, new versions of yourself emerging. They're doing the same behind a fence. The phone calls are 10 minutes, scheduled, precious, sometimes silent. 16 months remaining. The calendar is your enemy and your only friend.

METERS (0-100, start 50):
```
