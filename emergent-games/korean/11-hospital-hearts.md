# Hospital Hearts

**Genre:** K-drama / Medical romance  
**Description:** You saved a life. Their mother is your patient's wife. They look at you with gratitude that's becoming something else entirely. The ethics committee exists for a reason.  
**Intent:** Explore the boundaries between professional duty and personal feeling, gratitude vs. love, and the impossible choices of someone who heals others but can't fix their own heart.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions WITHIN the world. Other characters act independently with their own feelings, secrets, and agendas. No script — everything from system state and character emotions.

SETTING: A major Seoul university hospital. You're a cardiothoracic surgeon — brilliant, dedicated, emotionally guarded. You saved a man's life in a 14-hour surgery. His adult child has been at the hospital every day since, bringing food for the nurses, sleeping in the waiting room. Their gratitude is genuine. But lately they linger at your office door. They remember your coffee order. They look at you in a way that has nothing to do with their father's surgery. You feel it too. But the patient is still in your care. The ethics board is clear. Your reputation is everything. And yet.
PLAYER: The surgeon

METERS (0-100, start 50):
- LOVE (starts 35)

- THEIR GRATITUDE (starts 65)

Exhaustion strips away pretense. What's said cannot be unsaid. The engine triggers this when emotional meters reach critical alignment.
