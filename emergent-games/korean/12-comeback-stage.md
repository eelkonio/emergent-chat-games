# Comeback Stage

**Genre:** K-drama / Entertainment industry romance  
**Description:** You were an idol. You fell from grace. They were your biggest fan — now they're a producer with the power to give you a second chance. Professionalism is required. Chemistry is inevitable.  
**Intent:** Explore power dynamics, reinvention, the weight of public image, and whether two people can build something real when one holds the other's dream in their hands.

---
