# The Rivalry

**Genre:** Music / Competition Drama  
**Description:** Two bands from the same scene. Same city. Same venues. Same audience. You came up together — shared bills, borrowed gear, supported each other. Then they got the Pitchfork review. Then they got the late-night TV spot. Now they're headlining the venue you still open at. The press calls it "healthy rivalry." It doesn't feel healthy. Your guitarist is jealous. Your drummer thinks you should change your sound to compete. And their frontperson — who used to be your friend — just mentioned you in an interview. Not kindly.  
**Intent:** Explore professional jealousy in creative communities, whether competition inspires or corrupts, and the loneliness of watching peers succeed while you grind.
