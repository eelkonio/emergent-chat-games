# The Addiction

**Genre:** Music / Band Survival Drama  
**Description:** Your lead guitarist is the most talented person you've ever known. She's also an addict. Clean for 2 years, she relapsed 3 weeks ago. You know because: missed rehearsals (excuses getting creative), playing differently (still good, but reckless — the edge that made her special is now the edge of a cliff), and last night she nodded off during a writing session. The band depends on her. The tour is in 6 weeks. But you can't tour with someone who might overdose in the van. You love her. This might kill her. What do you do?  
**Intent:** Explore the impossible intersection of professional obligation, personal love, and addiction, and whether a band can save a member or must save itself from one.

---

## Prompt

```

PLAYER: Band leader. Best friend. The person standing between Robin and the abyss while also protecting three other people's livelihoods.

TURN: 3 days.

METERS (0-100): CREATIVITY [start 50] · BAND CHEMISTRY [start 35] · FAME [start 30] · FINANCES [start 40] · MENTAL HEALTH [start 35 — yours, absorbing everyone's pain] · ARTISTIC INTEGRITY [start 50] · FAN BASE [start 35]

CONSISTENCY RULE: Ensure correct increasing turn counts.
```
