# The Legacy

**Genre:** Music / Final Statement Drama  
**Description:** 30 years as a band. 8 albums. Hall of Fame. Stadiums. You're legends. And you know — without saying it — that this is the last album. Your drummer's hands aren't what they were. Your guitarist's hearing is going. You're 55 and the voice that carried a generation doesn't soar like it once did. But you have wisdom now. One last album. Make it worthy of everything that came before. Or: make something so different it confuses everyone. Or: make nothing and let the legacy stand.  
**Intent:** Explore aging in art, the weight of legacy on creation, and whether one final statement can capture 30 years of living and playing — and the courage to say goodbye.
