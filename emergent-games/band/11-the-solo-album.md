# The Solo Album

**Genre:** Music / Loyalty vs. Ambition Drama  
**Description:** You've written 12 songs that don't sound like the band. They're quieter. More personal. Different genre entirely. You want to make them — but not AS the band. A solo album. The word "solo" in a band context is a grenade. It signals: "I don't need you." Even if that's not what you mean. Even if you plan to come back. The band will read it as: beginning of the end. Because historically, it always is.  
**Intent:** Explore individual ambition within collective art, whether creative growth demands solo expression, and if a band can survive its members outgrowing it.

---

## Prompt

```

SETTING: You've been secretly writing outside the band's style for a year. 12 songs: folk-acoustic where your band is synth-rock. Personal where the band is anthemic. Quiet where the band is loud.
```
