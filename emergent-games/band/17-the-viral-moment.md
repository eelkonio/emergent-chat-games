# The Viral Moment

**Genre:** Music / Internet Age Drama  
**Description:** A 15-second clip of your song — the bridge of "Paper Chains" where you crack emotionally live — went viral on TikTok. 8 million views in 3 days. Your Spotify jumps from 5K to 200K monthly listeners overnight. Labels are calling. But: 8 million people heard 15 seconds of one song. They don't know the band. They know a MOMENT. How do you convert a moment into a career? And what happens when the algorithm moves on tomorrow?  
**Intent:** Explore virality as artistic challenge, the gap between attention and audience, and whether a career can be built on the internet's 15 seconds of grace.

---
