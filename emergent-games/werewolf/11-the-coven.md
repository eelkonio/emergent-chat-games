# The Coven

**Genre:** Social Deduction / Dark Fantasy  
**Description:** A circle of seven witches. You've practiced together for 12 years — sacred bond, shared power, deep trust. But someone has gone dark. The signs: a corrupted healing spell (patient got worse), tainted ritual wine (hallucinations during ceremony), and a summoning circle drawn in blood under the altar (none of you work with blood magic). One sister has turned. The corruption is spreading through your shared magical bond. Find her before the next full moon — or the entire coven falls into shadow.  
**Intent:** Explore betrayal within spiritual community, the corruption of sacred bonds, and how intimate trust creates the deepest vulnerability.

---
