# Future Self

**Genre:** Time Travel / Paranoid Thriller  
**Description:** You received a letter from yourself — dated 2037. It contains specific warnings: don't take the promotion, don't trust Daniel, leave the city before June. The handwriting is yours. The details are things only you would know. But future-you sounds desperate, contradictory, and possibly insane. Following the warnings helps sometimes and hurts other times. Is future-you protecting you — or manipulating you into creating their timeline?  
**Intent:** Explore self-trust across time, the paradox of predestination, and whether your future self is still "you" enough to deserve your obedience.

---
