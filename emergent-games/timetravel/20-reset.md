# Reset

**Genre:** Time Travel / Social Drama  
**Description:** You can reset any single conversation or event — rewind 5 minutes and redo it. Once per event. But people feel it. They don't remember the first version, but they feel an "echo" — a sense that something happened, a residual emotion from the erased timeline. Say something cruel, reset, say something kind — they'll feel warmth toward you AND an unexplained discomfort. The echoes accumulate. People who've been reset too many times start acting strangely around you.  
**Intent:** Explore micro-manipulation, the ethics of social do-overs, and how the emotional residue of erased choices builds into something no one can explain but everyone feels.
