# Time Prison

**Genre:** Time Travel / Psychological Drama  
**Description:** Temporal imprisonment: you've been sentenced to relive 2019 — the year you committed your crime. Every January 1st, you reset. You remember everything. No one else does. The sentence is 20 loops (years). You're on loop 7. But you can make different choices within the year. The system watches. Good behavior might shorten the sentence. Bad behavior extends it. The question: can you become a different person by reliving the context that made you who you are?  
**Intent:** Explore rehabilitation through repetition, whether environment determines choice, and the psychology of punishment with perfect memory.

---
