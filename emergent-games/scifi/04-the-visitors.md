# The Visitors

**Genre:** Science Fiction / First Contact  
**Description:** Twelve ships entered the solar system nine days ago, decelerating from 0.3c. They broadcast on all electromagnetic frequencies: mathematical sequences, then prime number patterns, then something that might be music — or echolocation — or prayer. They seem peaceful. They haven't fired a weapon. They've established orbit around Earth and transmitted what appears to be art. But their biology is ammonia-based, their communication is partly chemical, and their concept of "individual" may not exist. They want something. "Want" might not translate.  
**Intent:** Explores the fundamental challenge of communicating with genuinely alien intelligence — not "humans in masks" but minds so different that misunderstanding isn't a failure of translation but a feature of incompatible cognitive architectures. First contact isn't a conversation — it's two species trying to invent the concept of conversation.
