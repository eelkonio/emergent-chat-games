# The Dyson Swarm

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Generational Engineering Epic  
**Description:** The Heliosphere Project began 147 years ago. A megastructure — not a solid shell but a swarm of 40 billion solar collectors orbiting the sun, capturing its energy and beaming it to humanity's expanding civilization. You never chose this life. Your great-great-grandmother did. You were born into the project, trained for the project, and now you lead it. Generation Three. The collectors are 31% complete. Earth barely remembers why it started. The outer colonies depend on the energy that's already flowing. And something is wrong with the swarm — collectors in the inner ring are moving. Not drifting. Moving. Deliberately. In patterns no one programmed.  
**Intent:** Explores multigenerational commitment — what happens when a project outlives its founders, when the reason for building becomes mythology, and when the thing you're building develops properties nobody anticipated.
