# Diaspora

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Colony Survival  
**Description:** Gate technology proliferated faster than governance. Within one year, 200 million humans scattered across a thousand worlds — anyone with access to a gate and desperation enough to step through. No central authority. No supply chains. No cavalry. You lead 5,000 colonists on Kepler-442b — a world with oxygen, liquid water, and a biosphere entirely incompatible with Earth life. You can't eat anything here. It can't eat you. Every calorie must be grown in sealed environments from seed stock brought from Earth. The gate back home works — for now. But Earth has 999 other colonies to worry about. And this world has its own logic.  
**Intent:** Explores the immediate aftermath of a diaspora — when technology outpaces social infrastructure and communities must build civilization from scratch under alien conditions. The colony doesn't fail to explosions; it fails to nutrition deficits, social fractures, and the psychological weight of a sky with unfamiliar stars.
