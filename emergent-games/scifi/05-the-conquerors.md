# The Conquerors

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Existential War  
**Description:** They arrived without warning. Three cities — Lagos, Shanghai, São Paulo — ceased to exist in the first hour. Not bombed. Disintegrated. Molecular disassembly from orbit. Then a transmission in perfect English, Mandarin, Portuguese, and thirty other languages: "You have thirty days to evacuate the planet. We require the heavy elements in your crust. This is not negotiable. This is logistics." They are not cruel. They are not angry. They are mining contractors, and Earth is a resource deposit that happens to have residents. Conventional warfare is meaningless against entities that can unmake a city from orbit. But they made one mistake: they assumed we'd leave quietly.  
**Intent:** Explores survival against genuinely superior force — not through military victory but through finding the unexpected angle, the asymmetric advantage, the thing the invaders' models didn't account for. It asks: when conventional resistance is suicide, what does courage look like?
