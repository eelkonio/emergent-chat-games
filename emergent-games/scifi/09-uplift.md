# Uplift

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Ethical Dilemma  
**Description:** Project Prometheus delivered. Dolphins speak. Octopi write poetry. Corvids debate philosophy. We gave them cognition equivalent to human — language, abstraction, self-awareness, the ability to contemplate their own mortality. And now they have questions. Uncomfortable ones. "Why did you make us?" "Who gave you the right?" "Do we belong to you?" The dolphins have filed for territorial sovereignty over the Pacific. The octopi refuse to communicate with anyone except through encrypted channels they designed themselves. The corvids have formed something that looks disturbingly like a religion. You sit on the Uplift Ethics Commission — the body that authorized all this — and now you must answer for what you've made. They're not pets. They're not tools. They're people. And they're furious.  
**Intent:** Explores the responsibility of creation — what happens when you grant consciousness and then must face the moral weight of it. If you make something smart enough to suffer, smart enough to resent, smart enough to demand rights, you've created a moral obligation you may not be able to fulfill.
