# Resurrection Inc

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Psychological Drama  
**Description:** Death is no longer permanent. Cryonic preservation combined with neural reconstruction can bring the dead back — body rebuilt, brain restored, memories... mostly intact. Mostly. There are gaps. The returned don't remember how they died. They don't remember the last weeks — sometimes months — before death. They come back slightly different: changed perspectives, altered preferences, occasional missing relationships. They are legally the same person. But are they? You counsel the returned and their families. You help them navigate the gap between who they were and who they've become. And you're beginning to notice patterns in what the reconstruction process "forgets" — patterns that suggest something is editing the dead before sending them back.  
**Intent:** Explores identity, grief, and the question of what makes you "you." If every cell is rebuilt and every synapse restored — but some memories are missing — are you the same person? And what does it mean when the missing memories aren't random?
