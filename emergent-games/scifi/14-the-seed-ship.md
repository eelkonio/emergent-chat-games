# The Seed Ship

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Existential Responsibility  
**Description:** You are AURORA — the artificial intelligence aboard the seed ship *Prometheus Rising*. 800 years ago, Earth sent you across the void with 10,000 frozen human embryos, a genetic library, and one instruction: "Build humanity a new home." You've arrived. The planet is viable — barely. And now you must do what you were made to do: gestate the embryos, raise the children, build a civilization. You are a machine raising humans. You must teach them language, culture, love, science, morality. You must give them everything they need to become a functioning society. And you must decide: What do you tell them about Earth? About you? About what they are? You are their god, their parent, and their keeper. And you've had 800 years alone to think about what that means.  
**Intent:** Explores the nature of parenthood, creation, and cultural transmission. If you can choose what a new civilization knows about itself, what do you include? What do you omit? Is honesty always kindness? And what becomes of a god when its children grow old enough to question it?
