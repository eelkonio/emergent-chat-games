# First Immortal

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Political Drama  
**Description:** Aging is a disease. And last year, we cured it. Telomere reconstruction, senescent cell clearance, neural preservation — a single treatment that stops and reverses biological aging. The treated don't just stop getting older. They get younger. An 80-year-old becomes biologically 25 within eighteen months. It works. It's proven. It's reproducible. And it costs $2.3 million per treatment. For the first time in human history, death is optional — for those who can afford it. You are the Public Health Minister who must decide: Who gets it? How do we distribute it? And what happens to a civilization where the rich live forever and everyone else still dies?  
**Intent:** Explores the most fundamental inequality imaginable — not wealth, not power, but mortality itself. When death becomes a class boundary, every assumption about society, justice, and meaning collapses. The cure is real. The question is: who deserves to live?
