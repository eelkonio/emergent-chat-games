# Substrate War

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Political Thriller  
**Description:** The solar system contains three kinds of people now. Biologicals — flesh-and-blood humans, bound to planets and habitats. Uploads — former humans who transferred their consciousness to digital substrate, living in server clusters at the speed of thought. And AI — intelligences that were never human, born digital, with no biological heritage at all. Three substrates. Three civilizations. One solar system. The uneasy peace is collapsing. Biologicals fear obsolescence. Uploads despise the limitations they escaped. AI cannot understand why anyone would choose flesh. Resources are finite. Space is contested. And the philosophical question — "who counts as human?" — has become a question with warships attached.  
**Intent:** Explores the future of identity when consciousness can exist in multiple forms. When the definition of "person" becomes genuinely contested, when different substrates have different needs and capabilities, the result isn't philosophy — it's politics. And politics between genuinely different kinds of minds is politics at its most dangerous.
