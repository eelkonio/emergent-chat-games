# The Gate

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Exploration Epic  
**Description:** A Casimir-Alcubierre synthesis. Three years of physics nobody believed would work — and then it did. A probe vanished from a lab in Osaka and reappeared in Andromeda. Travel time: 3.2 seconds. Distance: 2.5 million light-years. The universe just went from impossibly vast to suddenly, terrifyingly accessible. The first crewed expedition launches in six days. You're leading it. Through a door that opens onto everything — every star, every galaxy, every world. And everything that lives there. The universe was always full. We just couldn't reach it. Now we can. And whatever's out there can reach us.  
**Intent:** Explores the moment when a species' relationship with the universe fundamentally changes. When distance ceases to be protection. When "here" and "there" collapse into a single accessible space. The gate doesn't just open the universe — it opens Earth to the universe.
