# Quantum Ghosts

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Psychological Mystery  
**Description:** Quantum entanglement doesn't just connect particles. It connects timelines. You discovered this six weeks ago when your lab equipment began receiving messages — from YOU. From versions of you in parallel branches of the quantum multiverse. They know things you don't. They've made choices you didn't. Some of them are better versions of you. Some are worse. And they all want something: to converge. To collapse the probability space and become one person again. But which one survives the collapse? The messages are getting more frequent. More urgent. More desperate. Some of your alternates are dying in their timelines. They want YOUR timeline. They want to be you. And you're beginning to receive memories that aren't yours — or ARE they?  
**Intent:** Explores identity when "you" becomes plural. If there are infinite versions of yourself, each equally real, each with their own history and loves and losses — who has the right to exist? When the multiverse is personal and immediate, the self becomes a contested resource.
