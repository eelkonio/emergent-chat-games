# Copy Number Seven

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Identity Thriller  
**Description:** Adult cloning became legal three years ago. Full biological copies with memory transfer up to the moment of scanning. You just received a legal notice: you are Copy Number Seven of Marcus Andreou, created fourteen months ago. Six other copies exist. The original exists. You have Marcus's memories up to the scan date — including memories of being "the real one." Your entire sense of self just became a legal fiction. And somewhere, six other people with your face, your memories, and your sense of being "the original" are having the same crisis.  
**Intent:** Explores identity, continuity of consciousness, and what makes a person "real" when biological uniqueness is eliminated. If seven people share the same memories and all feel equally authentic, who gets the life? The relationship? The career? The name? There may be no answer — only negotiation.
