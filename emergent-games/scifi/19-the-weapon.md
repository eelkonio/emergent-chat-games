# The Weapon

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Existential Security  
**Description:** It's called a nova trigger. A device the size of a suitcase that, when activated, initiates a chain reaction in any star's core — collapsing it into supernova within 72 hours. The physics was published by accident: a paper on stellar nucleosynthesis that contained, buried in its equations, the complete theoretical framework for stellar ignition. By the time it was retracted, 40,000 people had downloaded it. The first working prototype was built in a garage in São Paulo three weeks later. Any competent physicist with $50,000 in materials can build one. The secret is out. Stars can be killed. And anyone can do it. You sit on the emergency UN Security Council — and your job is to prevent the death of the sun. Any sun. All suns.  
**Intent:** Explores the ultimate proliferation nightmare — a weapon so devastating, so simple, and so available that no defense is possible. When anyone can destroy a solar system, the question isn't military — it's social. How does civilization survive when the capability for extinction is democratized?
