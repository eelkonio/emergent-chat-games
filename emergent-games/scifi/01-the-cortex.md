# The Cortex

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Cybernetic Thriller  
**Description:** Neural-link technology has gone mainstream. Direct brain-internet connectivity — thought-speed communication, instant knowledge access, shared sensory experiences. But the architecture that lets you browse the collective consciousness of eight billion minds also lets someone hack yours. Consciousness bleed between connected users is rising: memories that aren't yours, emotions that belong to strangers, dreams that were lived by someone else. The line between "you" and "everyone" is dissolving. And you helped build it.  
**Intent:** Explores the philosophical boundary of personal identity when minds become networked. If your thoughts are influenced by a system you can't disconnect from, are your decisions still yours? The neuroscience of self meets the network effects of technology — and the self might not survive the merger.
