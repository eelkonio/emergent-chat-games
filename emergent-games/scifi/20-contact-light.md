# Contact Light

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Engineering Mystery  
**Description:** The signal arrived from Proxima Centauri — four years travel time at lightspeed. It wasn't a greeting. It wasn't mathematics. It wasn't language. It was blueprints. Detailed, precise, unmistakable engineering schematics for a device of unknown purpose. Materials we recognize. Physics we can verify. Assembly we can perform. But no explanation. No context. No "hello." Just: build this. The schematics are elegant. The physics checks out. Every component is within our manufacturing capability. The device — whatever it is — can be built with 2024 technology. It's designed to be built by US. Specifically us. At exactly this level of technology. Someone knew what we could do and designed something we could make. And you are the engineer who must decide: do we build it? And what happens when we turn it on?  
**Intent:** Explores the ultimate engineering decision — building something whose purpose you cannot determine, designed by an intelligence that anticipated your exact capabilities. Trust without understanding. Creation without comprehension. The moment when curiosity and caution become indistinguishable from faith and fear.
