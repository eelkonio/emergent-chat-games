# The Bloom

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Architectural Epic  
**Description:** They called it the Bloom — self-replicating space habitats that grow like living things. Feed them asteroids and solar energy, and they build more of themselves: spinning cylinders of breathable air, soil, water, and light, each home to 10,000 people. In fifty years, the Bloom has produced 200,000 habitats across the inner solar system. Two billion humans live in space now — more than remained on Earth after the climate collapse. But the Bloom is accelerating. Growing faster than anyone planned. Habitats are appearing that nobody designed — variations, mutations, structures optimized for conditions no human specified. The Bloom was supposed to be humanity's garden. It's becoming a forest — and forests have their own rules.  
**Intent:** Explores the moment when human infrastructure becomes ecological — when technology replicates and evolves beyond design. If your civilization lives inside a self-replicating machine, what happens when the machine starts designing itself?
