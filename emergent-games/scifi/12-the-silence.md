# The Silence

**Genre:** Science Fiction / Archaeological Mystery  
**Description:** Fermi's question is answered. The galaxy teems with ruins. Dead civilizations on every world we reach — some fallen millennia ago, some mere centuries. Magnificent cities frozen in dust. Technology beyond our comprehension, silent and cold. And always the same final record: a species at the peak of its power, then nothing. No war. No asteroid. No disease. Just... cessation. One hundred and twelve dead civilizations catalogued so far. All different biologies. All different histories. All dead in the same way. You're on the 113th world, and this one is different. The ruins here are warm. The lights still flicker. Something on this world died very recently. And you think you can hear what killed it still moving in the deep structures.  
**Intent:** Explores the Great Filter as lived experience — not a theoretical concept but a physical reality you can walk through. What if the answer to "where is everybody?" is "dead, all of them, from the same thing" — and you're standing in the thing's last hunting ground?
