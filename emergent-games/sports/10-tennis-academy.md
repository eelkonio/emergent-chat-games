# Tennis Academy

**Genre:** Sports / Youth Development Drama  
**Description:** You run an elite tennis academy. Twelve talented teenagers. Their parents are worse than any opponent. One kid has genuine generational talent — but her father is destroying her love of the game. Another has less talent but perfect work ethic — and his mother spends every session in the viewing gallery making notes. You're developing humans first and champions second. The parents want it the other way around.  
**Intent:** Explore the ethics of elite youth development, toxic parent dynamics, and the tension between nurturing love of the game and producing competitive results.

---
