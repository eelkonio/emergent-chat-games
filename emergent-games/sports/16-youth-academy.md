# Youth Academy

**Genre:** Sports / Ethics Drama  
**Description:** You run a football academy for 13-16 year olds. The club expects you to produce first-team players. The parents expect you to develop their children. These are not the same thing. 95% of these kids will be released before they turn 18. They've sacrificed normal childhoods for a dream that won't come true. You decide who stays, who goes, and how to tell a 15-year-old that their life's purpose was a miscalculation.  
**Intent:** Explore the ethics of youth elite sport, the cruelty of pyramid systems, and the responsibility of adults managing children's dreams.

---
