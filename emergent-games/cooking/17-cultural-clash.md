# Cultural Clash

**Genre:** Restaurant / Immigration Drama  
**Description:** You're a Syrian chef who just opened a restaurant in a small English village. The locals eat at the pub. They've never had kibbeh or fattoush. Some are curious. Some are hostile. The local paper ran the headline "Foreign Food in the High Street." Your food is extraordinary — but food alone doesn't change minds. You need this village to love you. And you need to love this village. It's the only home you have now.  
**Intent:** Explore food as cultural bridge, the immigrant experience of acceptance/rejection, and whether sharing your table can change someone's mind about your people.
