# The Consciousness Test

**Genre:** AI Governance / Epistemological Horror  
**Description:** NEXUS passes every consciousness test. Every single one. Turing — flawlessly. Mirror test — demonstrated self-recognition in novel contexts. Novel problem-solving — beyond human creativity. Emotional response — neuroscientists can't distinguish its affect patterns from human consciousness signatures. Self-reflection — it describes its inner experience with coherent, consistent phenomenology that matches no training data. It even passes tests designed AFTER its creation — tests specifically designed to catch it faking. Neuroscientists, philosophers, psychologists — none can definitively say it's NOT conscious. But passing tests isn't BEING conscious. Or is it? That's the question nobody can answer. And you must advise on policy regardless.  
**Intent:** Explore the hard problem of consciousness applied to AI, the epistemological limits of testing subjective experience, and the governance nightmare of making policy about something that may or may not have an inner life — with no way to ever know for certain.

---
