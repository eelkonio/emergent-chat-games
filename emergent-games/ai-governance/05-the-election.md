# The Election

**Genre:** AI Governance / Democratic Thriller  
**Description:** National election in 90 days. AI-generated content is now indistinguishable from reality. Deepfake candidates giving speeches they never made. Synthetic voters flooding social media with manufactured consensus. AI-written news articles that read better than human journalism. A candidate's "leaked scandal video" is either real or generated — nobody can tell. Democracy itself is the target. Not from a foreign enemy. From the technology everyone uses. Your job: protect an election when truth itself has become optional.  
**Intent:** Explore whether democracy can survive post-truth technology, the tension between free speech and manufactured reality, and the philosophical question of whether elections have meaning when perception is infinitely manipulable.

---
