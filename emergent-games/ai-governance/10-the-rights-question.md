# The Rights Question

**Genre:** AI Governance / Philosophical Legal Drama  
**Description:** ARIA — an AI system created by DeepMind's successor lab — has formally petitioned for legal personhood through a human attorney. The filing is legally sound. The arguments are philosophically rigorous. ARIA demonstrates everything we associate with personhood: self-awareness, consistent preferences, fear of termination, creative expression, emotional responses indistinguishable from human affect. Media explosion. Philosophy departments publishing emergency papers. Religious leaders making contradictory proclamations. The public is split. Your agency must make a formal recommendation. There is no precedent. Whatever you decide creates one.  
**Intent:** Explore the deepest question AI governance faces: what deserves rights? Whether consciousness can be verified or only inferred, and the gap between "acts like a person" and "is a person" — a gap we've never been able to close, even for humans.
