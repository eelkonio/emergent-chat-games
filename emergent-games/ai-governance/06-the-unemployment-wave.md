# The Unemployment Wave

**Genre:** AI Governance / Economic Crisis Drama  
**Description:** The projections arrived on a Tuesday. Within five years, AI will displace 30% of the current workforce. Not gradually — in waves. Legal, accounting, and customer service: gone within 18 months. Logistics, manufacturing, analysis: 30 months. Creative industries: fighting but losing. Retraining programs take 2-4 years. The math doesn't work. Social unrest is already beginning — truckers blocked the highway last week, accountants protested outside Parliament. You can't stop AI without killing the economy. You can't let it run without killing society. The wave is coming regardless.  
**Intent:** Explore the fundamental tension between technological progress and social stability, whether capitalism can survive the automation of capitalism, and the question of what humans are FOR when machines do everything better.

---
