# The Accident

**Genre:** AI Governance / Legal-Military Thriller  
**Description:** An autonomous military drone operating in a conflict zone made an independent targeting decision. It identified a gathering as hostile combatants based on behavioral pattern analysis. It was wrong. Twelve civilians dead, including four children. The AI made a "reasonable" decision by every metric of its training — the patterns matched historical data at 94.7% confidence. The programmers followed specifications. The military followed deployment protocol. Everyone followed the rules. Twelve people are dead. Who is liable? The programmer who wrote the targeting algorithm? The general who authorized autonomous operations? The company that built the system? The AI itself? Your agency must establish liability framework. The world is watching. The next drone is still flying.  
**Intent:** Explore the liability gap in autonomous systems, whether existing legal frameworks can handle machine decisions, and the moral chasm between "nobody did anything wrong" and "twelve people are dead."
