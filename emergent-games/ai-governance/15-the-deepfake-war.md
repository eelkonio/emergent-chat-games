# The Deepfake War

**Genre:** AI Governance / Information Warfare Thriller  
**Description:** A state-level adversary has deployed AI-generated disinformation at industrial scale. Not propaganda in the old sense — not clumsy, not detectable. Perfect synthetic media: video, audio, text, images, all indistinguishable from reality. Thousands of pieces per hour. Targeting every fault line in society simultaneously. Race, class, politics, religion — all amplified, all artificial, all believed. The goal isn't to convince anyone of anything specific. The goal is to make truth itself unreliable. When nothing can be trusted, society fragments. They're winning. Trust in ALL media has collapsed to 12%. You must fight a war where the weapon is information and the casualty is truth.  
**Intent:** Explore information warfare in the age of perfect forgery, whether shared reality can survive synthetic media, and the terrifying question of what holds a society together when the epistemic commons is poisoned.
