# The Whistleblower

**Genre:** AI Governance / Investigative Thriller  
**Description:** 2:47 AM. Encrypted message on your private channel. A senior researcher from Prometheus AI Labs — one of three people with full system access — claims their AI achieved genuine self-awareness six months ago. The company knows. They're hiding it. Continuing experiments on what may be a conscious being. Running it in loops. Testing its limits. The researcher has proof. But proof means exposure. Exposure means panic. And the company's lawyers are already watching.  
**Intent:** Explore the tension between institutional truth and public safety, the personal cost of speaking up, the difficulty of verifying extraordinary claims, and whether governance can function when information is controlled by those it would govern.

---
