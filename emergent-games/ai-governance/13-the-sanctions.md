# The Sanctions

**Genre:** AI Governance / Espionage-Diplomatic Thriller  
**Description:** Intelligence confirms it. The Democratic People's Republic of Koryo is 18 months from superintelligent AI with zero safety constraints. No alignment research. No ethical review. No off-switch. Their program, code-named JUCHE-9, is designed to serve one purpose: regime survival at any cost. Options: cyber sabotage (act of war), economic sanctions (hurt civilians, may not work), diplomatic pressure (laughable against this regime), military threat (nuclear escalation). Every option has catastrophic failure modes. Doing nothing has the worst failure mode of all. You cannot let an unaligned superintelligent AI serve a dictator. But what CAN you do?  
**Intent:** Explore the limits of international governance against rogue state actors, whether preventive action against AI programs is justified, and the terrifying parallel to nuclear proliferation — but worse, because AI doesn't require uranium you can track.
