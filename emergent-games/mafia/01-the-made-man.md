# The Made Man

**Genre:** Organized Crime Drama  
**Description:** The ceremony is done. You pricked your finger, burned the saint's card, swore the oath. You're a made man now — untouchable by the rules. But the rules only protect you from outsiders. Inside the family, you're the newest, the lowest, the most expendable. Your capo wants results. The street wants respect. And your first real job is coming.  
**Intent:** Explore initiation into a system of power, the cost of belonging, and how far you'll go to earn the respect you were promised.

---
