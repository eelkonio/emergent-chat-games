# The Prohibition

**Genre:** Organized Crime Drama  
**Description:** It's 1924. Alcohol is illegal. America is thirsty. You run a three-truck operation from the Canadian border to Chicago. The money is extraordinary. The violence is escalating. Capone's people want a piece. The Feds want a scalp. Your partner is getting greedy. This is the birth of the American mob — and you're building it in real time.  
**Intent:** Explore the origin myth of organized crime, how prohibition creates empires, and the intoxication of building something from nothing in a lawless space.

---

## Prompt

