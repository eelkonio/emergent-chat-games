# The Heir Problem

**Genre:** Period Royal Court Drama  
**Description:** Five years married. No child. The court whispers. Your spouse's family grows impatient. The King has mentioned "the succession." You've tried everything the physicians suggest. Time is running out. Without an heir, your lands revert, your title dies, and your enemies inherit everything you've built. The solutions available are all dangerous.  
**Intent:** Explore the brutal intersection of biology and politics, the dehumanization of fertility as duty, and the desperate measures people take when a system demands the impossible.

---

## Prompt

```

PLAYER: The childless noble. Your body has become a political battlefield. The most private failure is the most public concern.
```
