# Korea Calculus

**Genre:** Geopolitics / Alliance  
**Description:** The US is reducing military exercises with South Korea to please North Korea's Kim. Seoul feels abandoned by its primary security guarantor. Alliance fraying from within.  
**Intent:** Experience the dilemma of a secondary ally when your protector makes deals about you without you. How dependency shapes strategic autonomy.
