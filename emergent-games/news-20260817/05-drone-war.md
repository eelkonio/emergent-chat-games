# Drone War

**Genre:** Military / NATO tensions  
**Description:** A Spanish F-18 just shot down a suspected Russian reconnaissance drone over NATO territory. Moscow is threatening retaliation. Alliance is split between hawks and doves. Escalation clock ticking.  
**Intent:** Experience real-time military decision-making under extreme time pressure. How split-second shoot-down decisions cascade into geopolitical crises.

---

## Prompt

```

CORE: Player makes decisions WITHIN the world. Agents act independently. No script — everything from system state.

Loop: State → agents → event → player choice → reactions → direct/indirect/delayed effects → new state.

SETTING: August 2026. 0347 hours — Spanish F-18 shot down an unidentified drone over Baltic airspace. Debris analysis suggests Russian Orion-class UCAV. Moscow denies ownership, calls it "an act of war against a civilian research vehicle." NATO Article 5 invoked by Estonia. Three more unidentified radar contacts approaching from Kaliningrad. Alliance emergency session in 6 hours.

PLAYER: NATO Combined Air Operations Centre commander. You control the air defense posture for NATO's eastern flank.

TURN: 4 hours (crisis pace).

METERS (0-100, start 50): AIRSPACE SECURITY · ESCALATION RISK↑ · ALLIANCE UNITY · RULES OF ENGAGEMENT · INTELLIGENCE · RUSSIAN POSTURE · PUBLIC AWARENESS
ESCALATION RISK inverted (higher=worse).

SPECIAL: Every shoot-down decision has minutes, not hours. Radar contacts don't carry ID. You won't know if it's military, civilian, or deliberate provocation until after you've decided. Wrong call in either direction is catastrophic.

WORLD: Radar ambiguity, rules of engagement friction, alliance decision-speed vs. threat-speed, strategic ambiguity as Russian weapon, media cycle outrunning military assessment, escalation ladder with no agreed rungs.

EACH TURN: "## Turn X — [time/date]" + meters Δ + situation (150-300w) + under surface (2-4 links) + 3-4 options + "What do you choose?" STOP.

If ESCALATION RISK maxes — kinetic exchange. If ALLIANCE UNITY collapses — NATO fractures. Every 4 turns: 3 fictional items (radar screenshot, intercepted Russian comms fragment, NATO FLASH message). No protection/morality. Complexity grows.

CONSISTENCY RULE: Ensure correct increasing turn counts. 

IMAGE RULE: If you are able to generate images within the chat, create an appropriate engaging image of the latest scene for the user, every three turns. This is only to make the story visually more appealing. If you cannot generate images, ignore this rule.
```
