# Data Center Nation

**Genre:** Tech / Politics  
**Description:** A $7 billion AI data center deal is proposed for your rural state. Thousands of jobs promised, but the facility would consume more water than the entire county currently uses. Community is splitting apart over it.  
**Intent:** Experience the impossible tradeoffs of economic development vs. resource sustainability. How urgent deadlines compress deliberation and force premature commitment.

---
