# Oil and Algorithm

**Genre:** Technology / Ethics  
**Description:** Your AI company's optimization tools make fossil fuel extraction 5% more efficient globally — a finding just published in Nature. Activists are protesting your offices. The board wants growth. Your engineers are threatening to quit.  
**Intent:** Experience the ethical trap of successful technology. When your product works brilliantly and that's precisely the problem. How complicity scales with capability.

---
