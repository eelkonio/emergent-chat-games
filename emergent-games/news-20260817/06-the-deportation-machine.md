# The Deportation Machine

**Genre:** Politics / Ethics  
**Description:** Federal ICE agents are operating in your county with body cameras after several fatal incidents. Your state passed 250 pro-migrant bills. Federal government demands full cooperation with mass deportation. You're caught between two legal systems.  
**Intent:** Experience the impossible position of local law enforcement when federal and state law directly conflict. How individuals become the pressure point where systems collide.
