# Vaccine Mandate

**Genre:** Public health / Politics  
**Description:** The president just split the MMR vaccine and reduced the childhood vaccination schedule. Measles outbreaks are beginning in schools. States must decide whether to follow federal guidance or protect children.  
**Intent:** Experience the collision between political power and scientific evidence. How public health officials navigate when the government itself becomes the health threat.
