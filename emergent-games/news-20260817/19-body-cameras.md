# Body Cameras

**Genre:** Civil rights / Accountability  
**Description:** After fatal ICE incidents, body cameras are mandated for all agents. The footage reveals abuses — but also exonerates some agents. Every side leaks selectively. The truth is there but weaponized by all.  
**Intent:** Experience the paradox of transparency as a tool for justice. How evidence becomes narrative, and how accountability organizations navigate when the same footage serves opposite agendas.

---
