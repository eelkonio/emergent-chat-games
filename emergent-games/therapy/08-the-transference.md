# The Transference

**Genre:** Therapy / Erotic Transference Drama  
**Description:** Elena, 32, has been your client for seven months. It started subtle — lingering eye contact, arriving early, mentioning she'd "thought about what you said all week." Then the gifts appeared: first a book she thought you'd like, then homemade cookies, then a scarf "that matched your eyes." She's started dressing differently for sessions — more carefully, more deliberately. Last week she said: "You're the only person who really sees me." This week, she's going to say something you can't ignore. The transference is textbook — she's recreating an early attachment pattern where love had to be earned through performance and pleasing. But knowing the theory doesn't make the moment less human, or less dangerous.  
**Intent:** Explores the razor's edge between therapeutic boundary and therapeutic relationship — how to name desire without shaming it, use it as clinical material without being cold, and maintain the frame that keeps both parties safe.
