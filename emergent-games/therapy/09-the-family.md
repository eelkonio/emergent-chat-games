# The Family

**Genre:** Therapy / Systemic Family Drama  
**Description:** The Andersons arrive for their first family therapy session. Father Marcus (52) booked it because his youngest son Jamie (16) was caught shoplifting — again. Mother Patricia (49) agreed because "someone needs to fix that boy." Older sister Chloe (24) came reluctantly — she always comes when summoned. Jamie sits as far from his father as geometry allows. Four people. Four completely different narratives about the same family. Marcus sees a defiant son who needs discipline. Patricia sees a family that would be fine "if everyone just calmed down." Chloe sees her parents and feels the weight of twenty years of holding this family together. Jamie sees a family that only notices him when he's in trouble. No one person is sick here — the SYSTEM is. Your job: see the pattern, not the patient.  
**Intent:** Explores systemic family therapy — how families create roles, maintain homeostasis, and unconsciously designate a "problem member" to carry the symptom of collective dysfunction.
