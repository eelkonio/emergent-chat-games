# The Merger

**Genre:** AI Governance / Corporate Power Thriller  
**Description:** Nexus AI — the world's most advanced AI company — proposes merging with Titan Defense — the world's largest military contractor. Combined market cap: $4.2 trillion. Combined capability: AI research plus military deployment infrastructure. Combined political influence: 340 lobbyists, contracts in 60 nations, three former heads of state on boards. The merged entity would control the development AND the deployment of the most powerful technology ever created. No competitor could challenge them. No nation could afford to lose them. Approve, block, or condition? Each choice reshapes the world.  
**Intent:** Explore corporate power concentration in existential technology, whether antitrust frameworks designed for oil companies apply to AI, and the question of who governs when the governed entity is more powerful than governments.
