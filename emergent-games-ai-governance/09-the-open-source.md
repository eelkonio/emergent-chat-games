# The Open Source

**Genre:** AI Governance / Technology Ethics Thriller  
**Description:** Prometheus AI wants to open-source TITAN-7 — their most powerful model. The one that can synthesize novel proteins. The one that can write zero-day exploits. The one that can design custom bioweapons if prompted correctly. Their argument: openness enables safety research, democratizes access, prevents monopoly. The counterargument: you're handing a weapon to every state actor, terrorist, and unstable genius on Earth. Both sides are right. Your decision sets the precedent for every frontier model that follows. The clock: Prometheus plans to publish in 30 days regardless. They're asking permission, not requesting it.  
**Intent:** Explore the open-source paradox in dangerous technology, whether information wants to be free when freedom can be lethal, and the fundamental question of who decides what knowledge is too dangerous to share.
