# The Legacy Platform

**Genre:** Influencer / Creator Simulation  
**Description:** You're 38. Your audience's average age: 22. The platform's new features are designed for 16-year-olds. Trends move faster than you can follow — and some of them make no sense to you. You still make good content. You still have your audience. But you feel the shift: you're becoming a legacy creator on a young platform. The new kids don't know your name. The algorithm prefers them. You're not old. But the internet says you are.  
**Intent:** Explore aging in youth-dominated spaces, how relevance shifts generationally, and whether maturity can compete with novelty on platforms built for the new.

---
