# The Colony

**Genre:** Space Trader / Frontier Sci-Fi  
**Description:** Hawthorne Colony, population 3,400. Three months from the nearest hub. Their supply ship didn't come last quarter. Or the quarter before. They're running on reserves and rationing. You have a hold full of supplies they desperately need. They can't pay market rate. But if you don't deliver, people will die before the next ship comes. The question is simple: what's a life worth in credits?  
**Intent:** Explore the moral economy of frontier supply, how isolation creates dependence, and the weight of being someone's only hope when you have bills of your own.

---
