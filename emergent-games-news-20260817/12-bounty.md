# Bounty

**Genre:** Military intelligence  
**Description:** Iran places a $30,000 bounty on US soldiers deployed in the region. Morale collapses as troops realize locals who once cooperated are now incentivized to target them. Force protection clashes with mission.  
**Intent:** Experience the corrosive effect of bounties on military operations. How trust evaporates when every local interaction becomes a potential threat calculation.

---

## Prompt

```

Loop: State → agents → event → player choice → reactions → direct/indirect/delayed effects → new state.
```
