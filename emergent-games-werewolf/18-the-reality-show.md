# The Reality Show

**Genre:** Social Deduction / Entertainment Thriller  
**Description:** "Survival Island" — 10 contestants, tropical island, audience vote eliminates one per week. You're a contestant. But you've figured it out: one "contestant" is actually a plant from the production company. They're not competing to win — they're there to manufacture drama, influence eliminations, and ensure the "right" people stay for ratings. Their vote is guided by producers through an earpiece hidden in their hair. The game is rigged. And if you expose the plant, either you're a hero or production eliminates YOU for breaking the show.  
**Intent:** Explore manufactured reality, the invisible hand of production in "unscripted" entertainment, and whether you can play a rigged game while trying to un-rig it.
